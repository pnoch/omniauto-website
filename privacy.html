Privacy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

OmniAuto runs on your phone and talks to the car through Android media and notification surfaces. There is no OmniAuto account and no first-party analytics server. Telemetry and crash reporting are off by default.

Data the app handles

  • Local audio metadata — title, artist, album, duration and artwork from the device MediaStore, used to play and display your library. Never uploaded by OmniAuto.
  • Lyrics lookup — when you open Lyrics, the track title, artist and duration are sent over HTTPS to LRCLIB, SimpMusic or KuGou to find synced or plain lyrics; OmniAuto does not store them server-side.
  • YouTube data — search queries, results and playback requests go through the bundled engine to YouTube. Sign-in cookies are stored in the app's WebView storage and can be cleared by signing out.
  • Navigation cues — Google Maps notification text is parsed on-device for the cluster. The last cue and the playback queue are stored locally so they can be restored.
  • Local history — search history, playback history and the current queue are stored on the device and can be cleared from the UI.

Opt-in data

  • Anonymous stats — when enabled, sends event names only (no titles, queries, identifiers or location) to the configured endpoint.
  • Crash reports — when enabled, sends stack traces to the configured Sentry DSN.

Both are off until you turn them on in Settings → App & Advanced, and can be turned off at any time.

Permissions and why

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO — read local music.
  • Notification listener access — read Google Maps navigation notifications.
  • Foreground service — keep playback and cue delivery alive while driving.
  • Internet — YouTube search and playback and optional updates or telemetry.

Controls

  • Clear search and playback history in the app.
  • Sign out of YouTube to clear its WebView cookies.
  • Disable anonymous stats and crash reports in Settings → App & Advanced.
  • Uninstalling removes app-private data (history, queue, cookies and tokens).

No ads, no selling of data, and no third-party analytics beyond the opt-in endpoints above. Questions: support@siamcreations.app.
